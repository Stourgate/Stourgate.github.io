**6. WHERE IN THE WORLD?**

The Bugle's cat, Timmy, has gone missing again. Where in the world is he this week? This week Timmy is one mile north of Stourgate, where in the world is he? Give your answer to the nearest town. All towns are in common use in the Oxford English Kids Book of Towns. In the event of a tie, jousting will be used to decide the winner.

Prize: luxury cruise for 18 people on the River Stour, alight Grove Ferry, disembark Stodmarsh. Fees may be applicable for oversize luggage, no pets except badgers. No cash equivalent. Competition promoted by Dave's Prizes, Dunton Industrial Estate, Dunton.
