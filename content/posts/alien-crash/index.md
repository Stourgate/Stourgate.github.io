**UFO crash**

Residents near the site of the long-proposed bypass have reported an unidentified object in a field off the ring road on Wednesday evening, though the council says it has no record of any incident. Several neighbours on the Fenwick Road estate said they heard a low humming noise shortly before 9pm, followed by what one described as a bright flash over the field. Kent Police confirmed they attended the scene but found nothing to report and have not opened an investigation.

**Bypass**

The bypass has been mired in controversy after councillor Barry Gainskill's plans to link the bypass to the A41. Labour demanded Gainskill's resignation after his proposal meant the cost of the road would increase from £14 million to £10 billion. The scheme has now been on the council's agenda for twenty years without a single sod being turned, and residents living near the proposed route say they have largely stopped expecting it to happen. A further council vote on the matter is scheduled for next month.

Mr Gately said that UFOs are surprisingly common in East Kent, and pointed to at least six other reported sightings in the area over the past decade, none of which were ever satisfactorily explained. 'I expect it is an alien who has travelled here from Alpha Centauri. Usually such aliens have ray guns so should be approached with caution.'
