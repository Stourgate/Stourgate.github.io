**New first-team manager**
Dunton Town have named Wayne Coulson as their new manager, filling the vacancy left by Colin Pratt's move to Blackhampton Wanderers earlier today.

Coulson departs Stourgate Town after two seasons in charge, having left the club just before the appointment of Ray Hutchings was announced. He becomes the third manager to change clubs within the Kent Large Moulded Fittings Division 2 in a single afternoon.

'It's a good move for me,' Coulson said. 'Dunton have real ambition. I wish Ray all the best at Stourgate, and Colin all the best at Blackhampton. They have both been a great help to me since I was released a few years ago now.'

A Dunton Town spokesperson said the club were 'delighted' to bring in a manager with Coulson's experience and said there was no need for any further safeguarding relating to Coulson's previous position, or his access to the youth setup.
