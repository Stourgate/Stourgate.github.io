Ray Barnes, Stourgate Town's longest-serving player, sat down with the Bugle this week to look back on a career spanning more than two decades at the club.

We asked him if he had any regrets. "Not really I mean I had a great time and all. Although I wish I'd got Helman back."

Steve Helman's wild tackle had badly injured Ray in a league cup match in 1992.

"I couldn't play for a year after that tackle. Bastard. I'd forgotten about that. I'm gonna get that ****ing ***"

At that point the interview was brought to a close. The Bugle has approached Mr Barnes for further comment.
