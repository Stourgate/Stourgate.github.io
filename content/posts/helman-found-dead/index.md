A man has been found dead outside the Sainsbury's superstore on Stourgate Road in what police are treating as an unexplained death.

The man has been named locally as Steve Helman, 58. Kent Police confirmed he was found in the store's loading bay area on Sunday morning by a member of staff and pronounced dead at the scene. Officers said he had sustained fatal injuries consistent with decapitation, and that the exact circumstances remain under investigation.

A spokesperson for Sainsbury's said the company was "supporting the police with their enquiries" and that the loading bay had been temporarily closed while an internal review was carried out. The store itself remained open to customers throughout Sunday.

Police have not confirmed whether the death is being treated as an accident. Anyone with information has been asked to contact Kent Police.
