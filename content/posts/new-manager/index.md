**New first-team manager**
Stourgate Town have confirmed the appointment of a new first-team manager, ending weeks of speculation. Ray Hutchings joins us from fellow Kent Large Moulded Fittings Division 2 side Blackhampton Terriers. Ray has managed for over ten years skilfully guiding teams between divisions in eight of those ten years. He hopes to repeat the promotion he gained with Blackhampton Wanderers in 2022. 

'Course I know Stourgate and the Warren well. I even got relegated there on the last day of the 2017, 2018 and 2021 seasons. So I suppose I am due some good luck! I am excited to get to work and that is not just with the football club but also outreach to the community'. 

**John Bradwell, welcomes Hutchings**
The club's owner, John Bradwell, welcomed Ray. 'Ray has been really big in local football in this area of Kent. He brings a wealth of experience. Some say this is a controversial decision due to his many relegations and legal issues but as far as I am concerned that is all behind him him and the future is bright. He got Blackhampton up on the first attempt and their subsequent relegation can't be put at his door since he was in jail for at least half the season.' 

**Andy Bracknell may or may not hate him**
Hutchings has kept all last years coaches in place surprisingly including first team coach Andy Bracknell. Andy worked with Ray in 2016 at GlamLake Terriers where they had a falling out and Andy paid a skywriting company to fly a plane trailing a banner that read 'Ray Hutchings is a knobhead'.

**New first-team manager**
Blackhampton Wanderers have appointed Colin Pratt as their new manager, just hours after Ray Hutchings left the club to take charge at Stourgate Town.

Pratt arrives from Dunton Town, where he spent four seasons, and takes over a Blackhampton side still adjusting to life after Hutchings.

'I know Colin well from the Kent Large Moulded Fittings set-up,' said Blackhampton chairman Derek Small. 'He did a solid job at Dunton and we're confident he can build on what Ray started here, even if Ray only started it about six hours before he left.'

Pratt said he was 'looking forward to the challenge' and confirmed he had already spoken to several of the Blackhampton players by phone, though not yet in person, as he was still driving back from Dunton at the time of the appointment.

**New first-team manager**
Dunton Town have named Wayne Coulson as their new manager, filling the vacancy left by Colin Pratt's move to Blackhampton Wanderers earlier today.

Coulson departs Stourgate Town after two seasons in charge, having left the club just before the appointment of Ray Hutchings was announced. He becomes the third manager to change clubs within the Kent Large Moulded Fittings Division 2 in a single afternoon.

'It's a good move for me,' Coulson said. 'Dunton have real ambition. I wish Ray all the best at Stourgate, and Colin all the best at Blackhampton, and I suppose whoever ends up at Stourgate next all the best as well, whenever that turns out to be.'

A Dunton Town spokesperson said the club were 'delighted' to bring in a manager with Coulson's experience, and confirmed there were no current plans to appoint anyone from Blackhampton, for now.
