Stourgate Parish Council has called for an independent investigation and the creation of a dedicated task force to look into the death of Steve Helman, despite East Kent Police already having an active murder investigation and a suspect in custody.

The proposal was raised by councillor Helen Saunders, who told the meeting that residents "deserved answers" and that the parish council should "take a lead role" in getting to the truth.

Other councillors pointed out that the parish council has no investigatory powers and an annual budget of £20,000 to cover all of its activities. Councillor Peter Wragg said the council "couldn't afford a task force if it wanted one, we can barely afford the hanging baskets."

The proposal was not put to a vote. Ms Saunders said she would raise the matter again at a future meeting.
